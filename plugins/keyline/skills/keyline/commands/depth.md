# `/keyline depth`

**Guide:** `domains/depth/guide.md` · **`DESIGN.md` section:** `## Surfaces` · **Procedure:** `commands/refine.md`

Makes every surface say where it sits — flat, raised, floating or modal — with each level one bundle of surface, edge, shadow and layer. Shadows from one light, dark themes that lift by lightening, scrims, gradient strokes, and translucency only where it earns its place, legible from its tint alone, with every fallback.

Typical finds: a card that should be a section; four shadow recipes for two levels; dark-mode cards that vanish because the shadow was the only cue; blur stacked on blur; text over glass that fails contrast against its worst backdrop; no `@supports` or reduced-transparency fallback. Deeper references: `elevation-recipes.md`, `translucent-materials.md`, `fallbacks-and-performance.md`.
