# `/keyline patterns`

**Guide:** `domains/patterns/guide.md` · **`DESIGN.md` section:** `## Patterns` · **Procedure:** `commands/refine.md` · **Alias:** `copy`

How the product behaves and what it says: short, sentence-case labels and verb buttons; a row's second line naming type and origin; one treatment for destructive actions, with confirmation scaled to the loss; an AI agent's work shown step by step with approvals inline; keyboard-first dense tools; optimistic changes that roll back; dashboards, forms and sign-in that ask for no more than they need; actions that answer at once and say when they're slow; inputs that forgive format; flows that show how near the end is and end on a designed screen; on landing pages and articles, elements that do what their form promises. A pattern kept in one place and broken in another is the finding.

Typical finds: "Click here to submit your form"; Title Case generated from keys; "OK" on a delete dialog; three different confirm treatments; an agent that goes silent while it works; a dashboard of stat tiles that answer nothing; a Save button that sits silent for two seconds; a phone field that rejects spaces; a wizard that ends on "Success". Feedback rounds follow the guide's `## Iterating on feedback`. Deeper references: `copy.md`, `actions-and-forms.md`, `agents-and-live-states.md`, `collections-and-dashboards.md`, `iteration.md`.
