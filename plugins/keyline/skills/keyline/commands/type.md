# `/keyline type`

**Guide:** `domains/type/guide.md` · **`DESIGN.md` section:** `## Type` · **Procedure:** `commands/refine.md`

Makes the hierarchy readable at a glance: every size belongs to a role, nothing outranks the heading it sits under, weights each have one job, leading and tracking are corrected for size, emphasis uses axes the system already has, numbers that align are tabular, and fonts load without moving the text.

Typical finds: a template opening a step above the rest; a lead larger than its heading; five weights where two would do; uppercase eyebrows over every section; body copy tracked; prices in proportional figures; imported Markdown on the browser's own ladder. Deeper references: `ladders.md`, `display-tier.md`, `prose-and-emphasis.md`, `numerals-and-loading.md`, `type-audit.md` (the sweep after a type change).
