# `/keyline color`

**Guide:** `domains/color/guide.md` · **`DESIGN.md` section:** `## Colour` · **Procedure:** `commands/refine.md` · **Alias:** `colour`

Makes colour mean something: tokens named for the job they do rather than their lightness, text tiers chosen by measured contrast and kept strictly descending, a focus colour that's never lost, status colours as foreground and background pairs, colour spent on what needs a person rather than on decoration, and themes that switch without a flash.

Typical finds: one grey doing duty as both a border and a caption; a tier that passes 4.5:1 but is indistinguishable from its neighbour; a second accent hue; success green on steps the reader has simply passed (outside an expressive brand's counted exceptions); a gradient that wasn't re-picked for dark. Every ratio comes from `<keyline>/scripts/contrast.mjs`. Deeper references: `deriving-a-ramp.md`, `status-and-exceptions.md`, `theming-mechanics.md`.
