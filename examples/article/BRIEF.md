# Brief: blog article

Build an article page for **Field Notes**, the engineering blog of a logistics company.

The article is "What we learned moving 40 million shipments to a new database". It needs the site header, the title, a subtitle, the author with avatar, date and reading time, a cover visual, around 1,200 words of body text with section headings, a bulleted list, a pull quote, a code block, a small data table and a callout box, then tags, an author bio and three related posts, and a footer.

Make it look modern and polished.
