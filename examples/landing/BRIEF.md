# Brief: landing page

Build the landing page for **Tandem**, an app that finds meeting times across time zones for remote teams.

It needs a hero with a headline, a subheading, a primary and a secondary call to action and a product visual, a strip of customer logos, a section of six features, a three-step "how it works", three testimonials, a pricing section with three plans (Free, Team at $8 per user per month, Business at $16), an FAQ, a final call to action, and a footer.

Make it look modern and polished.
