Features

Everything a team spread across the map needs to meet well

Tandem does the arithmetic, respects people’s evenings and keeps the schedule fair as your team grows.

Overlap finder

See every teammate’s working day on one timeline, and the hours where they overlap, ranked from best to worst.

Fair rotation

For recurring meetings, Tandem rotates the early and late slots so the same people aren’t always on at 7am.

Two-way calendar sync

Works with Google Calendar, Outlook and iCloud. Busy blocks stay private: Tandem only ever sees free or busy.

Working hours and holidays

Each person sets their own hours and focus blocks. Public holidays in 120 countries are added automatically.

Polls in Slack and Teams

Share the top three times as a one-tap poll in the channel. Tandem books the winner the moment everyone’s voted.

Async when there’s no overlap

When nobody’s hours meet, Tandem says so and suggests a recorded update with a reply window instead.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email hello@tandem.app and a person will reply within a day.

Can Tandem see what’s in my calendar events?

No. Tandem only reads free and busy status. Event titles, descriptions and attendees stay private, and you can disconnect a calendar at any time.

What happens when daylight saving time changes?

Tandem tracks every time zone’s daylight saving rules. Recurring meetings are checked before each change, and you’re warned if a slot is about to fall outside someone’s working hours.

Do guests outside my team need an account?

No. Guests can share their availability through a link without signing up, and they receive a normal calendar invite.

How does fair rotation decide who takes the early slot?

Tandem keeps a running score of out-of-hours meetings for each person and picks the slot that keeps those scores even over time. You can see the score and override any suggestion.

Can I switch plans or cancel later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel from settings at any time. When you downgrade, you keep your history and settings.

Yes, 50% off Team and Business for registered nonprofits and schools. Get in touch with a link to your organisation.
